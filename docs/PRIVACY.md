# Privacy Policy: Vision AI (DRAFT, have a lawyer check it before you sell)

Last updated: 2026-10-27. Publisher: Agniv Sarkar.

**Short version:** Vision AI uses your webcam to see your hand. The camera picture stays on your PC. We do not record it, save it, or send it anywhere.

## What the app does with the camera
- The picture is processed live, on your PC, to find the position of your hand.
- Pictures are not saved to disk and are not uploaded. They are thrown away as soon as they are processed.
- The hand-tracking model (Google MediaPipe) runs on your PC, not in the cloud.

## What is stored on your PC
- Your settings, your finger calibration numbers, and a license key (if you entered one), in `%APPDATA%\VisionAI`.
- A log file with technical messages (no pictures) in `%APPDATA%\VisionAI\logs`.
You can delete the folder at any time. Uninstalling the app does not delete it automatically.

## What is sent over the internet
Nothing, at the moment. License keys are checked on your PC. (If online license checks or update checks are added later, this page will say exactly what is sent.)
